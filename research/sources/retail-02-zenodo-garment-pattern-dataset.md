## Description

The Dataset of 3D Garments with Sewing patterns contains more than 23500 3D garment models with their corresponding sewing patterns, each representing unique garment design. The upload is organized by type for the 12 garment types of the train group, and the [test.zip](https://zenodo.org/api/files/82d24e49-a652-46d2-9d9f-7197ec4addc5/test.zip?versionId=caf48bdc-cf01-444d-a05f-74942a7483b5) contains garments of 9 types from the test group.

The detailed documentation of the dataset is provided in the accompanying [Datasheet](https://zenodo.org/api/files/82d24e49-a652-46d2-9d9f-7197ec4addc5/Datasheet%20for%20Dataset%20of%203D%20Garments%20with%20Sewing%20Patterns%20v1..pdf?versionId=c27d0083-335f-4c0f-9c15-cc84361c1677).

Garment samples are obtained using the Garment Pattern Generator (available on [GitHub](https://github.com/maria-korosteleva/Garment-Pattern-Generator)) as presented in the paper [Generating Datasets of 3D Garments with Sewing Patterns](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Pq8FBz0gZHY)( _**NeurIPS 2021 Track on Datasets and Benchmarks**_)

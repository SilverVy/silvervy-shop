# Image usage

What can I use the images for?

Written by Sophia Soring
October 28, 2025

Brands decide which retailers may access their image material. Brands also have the option of restricting use to certain usage **channels** (print, e-commerce, social media, etc.) and the **period of use**.

In the terms and conditions, the retailer agrees to comply with the usage rights specified when downloading the material. Retailers may use all image material displayed to them within the scope of these restrictions.

* * *

- All brands indicate in which marketing channels the respective motif may be used. You can see the usage **Channels** directly in the small window next to the respective image.
